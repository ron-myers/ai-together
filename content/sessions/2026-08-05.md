## Next level: connect Claude to your email

```yaml

presenter: Michael Easter
takeaway: Turn on the Gmail connector and ask Claude about your inbox without forwarding a thing
links:
  - label: Claude connectors
    url: https://claude.ai
```

For people already comfortable with Claude: the Gmail connector lets Claude read
your email so you can ask it to find, summarise, or draft from what is already in
your inbox. Connect once, then ask.

## Going deeper: image style guides with JSON

```yaml
kind: advanced
presenter: Michael Easter
takeaway: Turn an image into a JSON style guide, then reuse it to generate new images that stay on-brand
```

A step further into images. We took an existing image, had the model describe it
as a JSON style guide — colours, mood, composition, lighting — then reused that
JSON to generate a new image that is unique but clearly from the same family.
Handy for keeping a consistent look across a set.
