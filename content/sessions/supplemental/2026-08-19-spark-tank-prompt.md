### Backround

Please read the spreadsheet, Scorecard-Actual.xlsx at [1]. These are the results of a pitch competition, Spark Tank 4.0, which is for tech entrepreneurs, in the style of Shark Tank. The competition had 3 judges who scored with 5 criteria from 1-10. There were 7 participants.

### Goal

Provide a feedback visualization PDF for each participant. Each feedback PDF should summarize their score for each criterion, across all judges, with the following:

* green traffic light (with text "good") for 75% or better
* yellow traffic light (with text "fair") for 65 % to 74%
* red traffic light (with text "poor") for < 65%

The PDF files should be written to [1] with the form `scorecard-summary-X.pdf` where `X` is the player initials as indicated in the spreadsheet.
