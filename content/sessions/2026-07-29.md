Week one, and the room is already full. We are starting slow on purpose — get comfortable first, go deeper later.

## Welcome

```yaml
kind: welcome
presenter: Michael Easter
takeaway: Come as you are, try one thing, and ask anything
```

We are taking the first few weeks slowly. A few ground rules to set the tone:

- Nothing to prepare. Show up, grab a chair, say your name.
- No question is too basic. If you are wondering it, someone else is too.
- We share to help, not to show off.
- Try one thing, not ten. Leave with a single next step.
- Jump in whenever. This is a room, not a lecture.

## Share a win

```yaml
kind: win
```

Open floor. Anyone with a small win from the week — a tool, a shortcut, a few
minutes saved. Keep it to two minutes so everyone gets a turn.

## Starter topic: generate your voice

```yaml
kind: starter
presenter: Ron Myers
takeaway: Paste your own writing into actmorehuman.com to get a voice profile you can reuse in any prompt
links:
  - label: actmorehuman.com
    url: https://actmorehuman.com
```

The fastest way to stop AI sounding like AI: give it your voice. We used
actmorehuman.com to turn a few samples of your own writing into a short voice
profile, then pasted that profile into a prompt so the output reads like you
wrote it.

## Next level: connect Claude to your Google Drive

```yaml
kind: intermediate
presenter: Ron Myers
takeaway: Turn on the Google Drive connector once, then ask Claude about your own files — no copy and paste
links:
  - label: Claude connectors
    url: https://claude.ai
```

For people already using Claude: the Google Drive connector lets Claude read the
docs and sheets already in your Drive. Connect it once, then ask questions about
your real files instead of pasting them in.

## Going deeper: build an app with zero code

```yaml
kind: advanced
presenter: Ron Myers
takeaway: Describe the app in plain language and base44 builds it — no code
links:
  - label: base44
    url: https://base44.com
```

A first look at building without writing code. We used base44 to build a small
feedback app for the building and the space here at The Foundry — describe what
you want, it builds it. Happy to share it with any groups that are looking for
the same.
